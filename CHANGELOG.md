## 2.25.0 "Save as picture"

The Team page and the member pages can now save nice information cards as .png pictures, ready to post in Discord or on socials.

- **Five kinds of card**, each 1200 x 675 with a big headline, one big number and a short punchy line made from the data (no AI): a **leaderboard** (top drivers, best laps, gaps, iRating and date, with the leader highlighted), a **head-to-head** ("A vs B at a track": lap times, the gap, corners won each and who is ahead overall), a **corner king** (who owns the corner and by how much, with speed in your Settings > Units unit), a **driver snapshot** (tracks, best laps, how steady, last raced) and a **new team best** card ("Robin set a new team best at Spa: 2:18.979").

- **Where it goes.** The picture is saved in the shares folder inside your reviews folder (for example Documents\Debrief Reviews\shares\spa-acme-gt3-leaderboard.png). Debrief says exactly where and has an "Open folder" button. It never writes over an earlier picture: a second one gets -2 in its name.
- The team name on a card comes from your Garage 61 team; the footer says "Debrief by Black Magik Studios". Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Not proven yet: the pictures were drawn in a test browser with stand-in team data. Fonts, and the Save and Open folder buttons inside the Windows window, still need a look on your PC with real Garage 61 data.

## 2.24.1

Set-Up Lab's automatic advice is much more careful: it only blames the setup when the evidence is strong.

An independent statistics check found the 2.23 advice called "understeer, Likely" on noisy laps from a perfectly good car far too often. It now stays quiet unless the evidence is strong.

- **Setup calls must pass a real test.** Understeer and oversteer are only called the setup when the Balance map's own luck check (which allows for the error in learning how the car normally steers) also calls at least two of the corners. Instability and traction must beat chance, corrected for the number of things checked. On laps with nothing wrong, false setup calls went from 15 to 100 percent of sessions in the noisy cases down to none in our test sets.
- **Noisy yaw signal.** Blips from bumps are smoothed out before the car's normal steering is learned, and a second way of learning it is compared; if the two disagree, the signal is too noisy and nothing is called a push or a rotation (it says so).
- **Entry is driving advice, never a setup verdict.** A corner entry is compared with your own typical entry, so a problem on every entry cancels out. It is now worded "relative to your typical entry" and points to the corner coach.
- **Sureness is honest.** It rests on the lap share over all corners of the group, not just the highest-looking ones. A slide measured over a short stretch (not one sample) counts for traction. If the Balance map says a group is neutral, it is capped at A hint. "Agree" on how it felt never raises a read above A hint.
- **A corner that swings** (understeer on some laps, oversteer on others) is driving, not setup.
- **Diff ramp suggestions** respect the "don't undo your last change" guard.
- **Screen fixes.** A card that proposes a change is titled "Next change: ..." ("No setup change" only when there is none). "Make it in the iRacing garage" only shows above setup cards. When laps exist but can't be used, it says why (for example "52 laps, but none has yaw-rate data"). "Not what I felt" asks what you did feel (understeer, oversteer, nervous entry, no grip on exit, fine) and keeps it. When the series' setup rules are unknown, setup ideas wait for a one-tap "Open setup? Yes / Fixed"; choosing Fixed switches the card to in-car dials or driving.
- Not proven yet: all tests use stand-in laps. Real Garage 61 and iRacing files may need the thresholds nudged (for example, the noisy-yaw check may be too cautious on a real car).

## 2.24.0 "Team sync"

Refresh team data is now a real sync of your teams' shared data, the way your own history sync works: it keeps a copy of your teammates' practice, qualifying and race laps on this PC, and the member pages and the reference picker read that copy.

- **What Refresh does now.** After the Team page is rebuilt, Debrief asks Garage 61 for each of your teams' driving statistics (one request per team; first time the last 180 days) to see who drove which track, car and day. It then fetches only the laps that are new, per teammate, at most 10 tracks per request, practice, qualifying and race together, unclean laps included and marked. Nothing already saved is downloaded again, and a repeat Refresh with no new driving costs one request per team.
- **Telemetry.** The best lap of each teammate at each track and car, up to 25 per Refresh, never twice. A lap Garage 61 won't share is remembered with the reason and asked for again after 30 days.
- **Gentle on Garage 61.** Requests are 3 seconds apart. After two "slow down" answers it stops and says "Synced 6 of 14 tracks; Refresh again to continue", and the next Refresh picks up where it left off.
- **Member pages.** Each teammate's page now lists their synced sessions (date, track, car, type, laps, best, typical lap), says in plain words how steady their clean laps are, and shows when they were last synced. The Team page's notes list one line per member: "Name: N tracks, M laps, last synced date".
- **Teammate laps as references.** "Compare with a teammate's lap" (Practice and the Coach) now lists the synced laps, with a "Use as my reference" button when their telemetry is saved.
- **Every gap says why**: "Garage 61 hasn't shared telemetry for this lap", "Not synced yet: press Refresh team data", "Not a clean lap".
- **Tester fixes.** The Team page now lists every team by its Garage 61 name with its member count, or says "Garage 61 didn't share a member list; members come from laps instead". "Same race" says "No teammates were in this race" when only you were there, names teammates in other cars with their car, and "1 driver" is singular. Prepare's "Your laps here" now says whether teammates have laps at that track (from the team library) or why not. Review options lists which teammates and cars "Comparison laps" could use. Settings > Team lists your Garage 61 teams with a show/hide tick (a hidden team is not synced) and when each was last synced.
- Teams and members still come only from your own Garage 61 teams.

Not proven yet: tested only against a stand-in Garage 61. The real team statistics rows and how many laps a real team has (so how long a first sync takes) need a real Refresh on your PC. Using a synced Garage 61 telemetry lap as the coach's reference, and the sessions table on the member page, have not been seen in the real window.

## 2.23.0

Set-Up Lab now gives setup advice on its own, for any car and track. There is no button: open Set-Up Lab (or a Practice session) and it has already read your laps.

- **Car balance: what to try next** is a new section at the top of Set-Up Lab. Debrief reads your clean laps corner by corner (way in, middle, way out) for understeer, oversteer, a car that is unsettled on the way in, and poor traction on the way out. It works out how this car normally steers from your own laps, so nothing is set per car.
- **Setup or driving?** A problem is called the setup only when it shows on most of your clean laps and in several corners of the same kind. If it comes and goes from lap to lap, or shows in one corner only, it says it looks like driving and points you to the corner coach. Every item says how sure it is: Sure, Likely, A hint or A guess, the same words as everywhere else.
- **One change at a time, ranked.** Each setup problem gets one change with a plain reason, what to expect and what to watch for, for example "Mid-corner understeer in slow corners on 9 of 11 laps: soften the front anti-roll bar one step." It draws on anti-roll bars, springs, ride height and rake, wing, differential, brake bias, traction control, tyre pressures and dampers, only for settings your car has. It never pulls the same part two ways and never simply undoes your last change unless the laps showed that change slower. In a fixed-setup series it only offers brake bias, traction control and ABS where the car has the dial, otherwise driving advice. You make the change by hand in the iRacing garage.
- **A short card on the Practice session page**: "Car balance: mid-corner understeer in slow corners (Likely). Try: ...". Every empty state says why, for example "Not enough clean laps yet: 4 of 6".
- **How did the car feel?** Under each item: Agree or Not what I felt. Your answer is kept on this PC and moves how sure Debrief is by one step next time.
- **Setup history.** What was suggested is kept for each car and track. After your next stints Set-Up Lab says whether you did it and what its own comparison showed (faster, slower or no clear difference), and whether the problem still shows. Nothing new is measured for this.
- Needs the steering, speed, throttle, brake, yaw rate and sideways g channels iRacing already saves. Wheelspin is also read from the four wheel-speed channels when the file has them; without them it is judged from how the car turns and speeds up instead.

**Not proven yet:** every number here (how big a wobble or a slide must be before it counts, how much sooner it is called setup than driving) was tuned on made-up laps, not on real telemetry. Real McLaren, Porsche and Mustang files may need the limits nudged. Lap traffic is only left out if the file marks it. The advice rules are conventional ideas and are untested on your laps, so the next stints are the test. Nothing has been checked on Windows.

## 2.20.0

Corner notes, and a coach that helps you learn a track before you have a reference lap.

- **Corner notes.** While you drive, hold **Ctrl+Shift+V** (or a wheel button you teach it under Settings on the Coach page: press **Learn**, then the button) and say a short note, like "brake later here". Let go to save it. A rising tone says it is listening, a falling tone says it is saved, and one low tone says it was thrown away (held under 0.4 seconds, or no speech heard). A note is at most 8 seconds. The coach says nothing while you record. Your voice is recorded from the default Windows microphone, only while the button is held, then the silence is trimmed, rumble is filtered out and the level is set to the same loudness as the coach's voices. Notes are kept on this PC in a `notes` folder next to your settings (never in iRacing's folders). A note said on the straight before a corner belongs to the next corner; one said in a corner belongs to that corner.
- **Your notes are played back before their corners**, ending about a second before the braking point, the way a brake marker is: never under braking, never in a corner, and dropped if it can't finish in time. One note per corner per lap (the newest that is switched on). They follow a learning plan: on each of your next 5 laps after you recorded it, then every third lap, then only on laps where that corner lost time against your reference. They respect Mute and "Only when I ask", are quiet on lap 1 of a race, and don't play in races at all unless you turned coaching in races on. **Rehearse with a session** plays them too, without counting them. There is a new coaching level, **Notes only**, and a switch under Settings to turn the notes off.
- **Practice > Corner notes** lists your notes by track, then turn, with when each was recorded, the lap and how many times it was played. For each: **Play**, **Re-record**, move it to another turn, switch it **On** or off, or **Delete** it (a deleted note waits in a trash list for 30 days, with **Restore**). **Copy summary** copies one line per note, like "Spa-Francorchamps, Turn 5, lap 4: note 3.1 s (played 5 times)". It never includes what you said.
- **Coach page:** a **Mic test** (records 3 seconds, plays it back, with a live level meter), a red **Recording** light whenever the microphone is in use, and your notes for the track you are on.
- **Learning the track.** With no reference lap yet, your first clean lap becomes the reference by itself, and each quicker clean lap replaces it. An off-track or incident lap never does. After that the coach compares each corner with your own best so far in this session: "T5, 3 down on your best" (speeds follow Settings > Units), "T3, braked 15 metres later" or earlier (10 metres or more, to the nearest 5), "T4, off track and 5 faster in" when you went off and went in faster than on your best, and "T5, on your best" once you put a corner right. The same corner isn't repeated lap after lap, and the usual limits on cues a lap and the gap between them still apply. **Ctrl+Shift+L**, a wheel button you teach it, or the **Use my last lap** button makes the lap you just finished the reference (only if it was clean). The Coach page says "Learning the track: 2 clean laps yet" until there is a reference, and shows your best so far after that. Settings has a switch for these words.
- **All six voices say the new words:** 60 new clips each ("three down on your best", "off track", "faster in", "braked fifteen metres later", "on your best" and the turn numbers they follow), made the same way as the others, at the same loudness and speed; the clips each voice already had are unchanged. Each voice still covers 100% of the coach's phrases.
- What can only be proven on a Windows PC with a microphone: the real microphone, the wheel button held down, and the timing in the car. Everything else is tested with stand-ins.

## 2.18.0

- **Car vs car** (new page, after Racing in the sidebar): pick a track and class (any class at any track: GT4 at Zolder, GT3 at Spa) and see every car of that class side by side: drivers, typical race lap, best lap, the gap for drivers of similar iRating, and how sure that gap is.
- **The verdict is careful.** "Drivers of similar iRating were about 0.4 s a lap quicker in the X than in the Y" is only said when each car has at least 15 drivers with an iRating (counted in the iRating bands both cars have) and a shuffle test, counting every pair of cars at once, says chance alone would show a gap that big less than 1 time in 20. Under it, one plain line: car choice and driver skill can't be fully separated. How sure each gap is reads **Unlikely to be chance**, **Likely** (less than 1 time in 5) or **Not enough to tell**. The typical and best laps are raw (they include who drove each car); only the gap is adjusted.
