    Get a free Garage 61 account

    Sign up at garage61.net and connect your iRacing account there. To compare laps with your teammates, ask to join the Midfield Menace team on Garage 61.

  2. 2

    Ask for your personal token

    Go to garage61.net/developer and request a personal API token. It's free for personal, non-commercial use. Garage 61 looks at each request by hand, so it can take a little while to arrive.

  3. 3

    Paste your token here

    It's saved on this PC only, and Debrief never shows it again. Debrief then checks the connection (about six Garage 61 requests).

            Review options

            Used in the race summary ("Sir, you completed..."). Up to 20 characters. Leave it empty for a plain "You completed...". New reviews use it.

            History sync

            Debrief keeps a copy of all your Garage 61 laps (races, qualifying and practice). The first sync downloads everything, paced at about one request a second, so it can take a while; you can keep using Debrief, and it carries on next time if you close it. After that, each sync only asks for new laps.

            Team

            The Garage 61 team the Team hub and Prep pages compare you with.

            iRacing (optional)

            iRacing's own data (official finishing positions, iRating and safety rating changes) needs API credentials that iRacing issues on request to individual developers. You don't need them: you can type a race result into any review. If iRacing gives you a client ID and secret, paste them here and Debrief will use them once that feature is switched on. They're saved in settings.toml on this PC and never shown again.
