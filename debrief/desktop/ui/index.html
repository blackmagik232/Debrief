    Connect Garage 61 for race reviews

    1. 1

      Get a free Garage 61 account

      Sign up at garage61.net and connect your iRacing account there. If you're on a team, ask a teammate to invite you to it on Garage 61 so you can compare laps.

    2. 2

      Ask for your personal access token

      Go to garage61.net/developer and request a personal access token. It's free for personal, non-commercial use. Garage 61 looks at each request by hand, so it can take a little while to arrive.

                        Last cues

                          Rehearse with a session

                          Plays one of your Practice sessions through the coach as if you were driving it: the same voice, sound device and rules. Hear what it would say before you go on track. Not while you coach live.

                          At 2x and 4x the words still play at their own speed, so the car covers 2 or 4 times as much track while a cue is said. The coach allows for that (every cue still ends before its brake point), so fewer cues fit than at 1x. Next lap and Next cue skip ahead with nothing said; Next cue looks 3 laps ahead at most. Use 1x to hear exactly what you would hear in the car.

                          Voice and sound

                          Which voice, which headset, audio delay

                          Pick the headset you use in iRacing. Windows default follows whatever Windows plays through.

                          Audio delay

                          Measured: not yet

                          How long Windows takes to start a sound on the chosen device. The coach starts each cue that much earlier. A Bluetooth headset adds delay that Windows can't see.

                          Add your own voice pack

                          A Debrief voice pack is a folder (or a .zip of one) with manifest.json and .wav clips. Debrief checks it before adding it: only sound clips, nothing that runs.

                                Review options

                                Laps from your teammates and drivers you follow on Garage 61.

                                A name or title the race summary starts with (for example "Alex, you completed..."). Up to 20 characters. Leave it empty for a plain "You completed...". New reviews use it.

                                Units

                                Every page, every review and the coach's voice use this. Lap times are always minutes:seconds.thousandths, like 1:35.849.

                                History sync

                                Debrief keeps a copy of all your Garage 61 laps (races, qualifying and practice). The first sync downloads everything at a gentle pace, so it can take a while; you can keep using Debrief, and it carries on next time if you close it. After that, each sync only asks for new laps.

                                Setup Lab and Coach

                                Both read the telemetry files iRacing saves (Documents\iRacing\telemetry). Debrief only reads them, never changes them. If iRacing saves them somewhere else, paste that folder here; leave it empty to use the usual one.

                                Updates

                                –

                                Debrief checks GitHub for a newer version once a day. Updating downloads the installer, checks it arrived intact, installs it and reopens Debrief. Your settings, reviews and history are kept.

                                Updates are checked by SHA-256 checksum: a fingerprint that proves the update file wasn't changed. Signed updates switch on once a signing key is published.

Nothing goes through the internet except your sync app.

                                Discord

                                Off

                                Your saved Discord webhook can't be opened on this Windows account. Paste it again.

                                A Discord webhook is a link that lets Debrief post into one Discord channel. Post a review's share card to the Midfield Menace Discord channel. In Discord: channel settings > Integrations > Webhooks > Copy Webhook URL, then paste it here. It's saved on this PC and Debrief never shows it again. Making a webhook needs the Manage Webhooks permission: if you don't have it, ask your team's Discord admin to make one and send you the link.

                                About

                                Debrief

                                Debrief · by Black Magik Studios. © 2026 Black Magik Studios.

                                Garage 61 asks apps to go easy on it, so Debrief makes about one request a second and reuses what it already has. Checking your token uses about six requests, a review or prep page a handful, and a first team refresh up to about 60.

                                Need help with a problem? Copy diagnostics puts Debrief's version, what it has saved and the end of its log on the clipboard, with your user name, token, webhook and any e-mail addresses taken out. Paste it to whoever is helping.

                                Race result

                                From the iRacing results page. Only the finishing position is needed; leave the rest blank if you like.

                                Incidents by lap

                                Optional. The review's race craft timeline marks these laps.

                                Link RaceCap

                                Debrief asks Garage 61 which RaceCap race this is. If it can't tell, type the race ID (the number at the start of the race's folder in RaceCap).

                                Scan this race

                                Load this race's replay in iRacing first. Debrief fast-forwards through the race once, usually a few minutes, and records nothing. It adds a race log of every pass, fight, incident and pit stop to this review.

                                No OBS or recording is needed. Just leave iRacing alone until it finishes. Your replay goes back to where it was.

                                Read a rival's lap

                                Load this race's replay in iRacing first. Debrief watches this driver's fastest clean lap at normal speed with the camera on them, so it takes about one lap, and records nothing.

                                The lap is added to Compare as a rival, to look at only. iRacing gives no pedals for other cars, so it has speed, gear and steering but no throttle or brake, and it never changes your coaching or time to find. Your replay and camera go back to where they were, paused.

                                    Debrief posts a review's share card to your team's Discord channel through a webhook link. You paste that link once in Settings.
